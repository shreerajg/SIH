Plain-Summary: A demonstration toy safety standard covering the physical dangers of a toy - parts that can come off and be swallowed, sharp edges, cords that can strangle, and whether the toy survives being dropped. Chemical safety is dealt with separately in DEMO-STD-006.

1 Scope
1.1 This standard specifies requirements and test methods relating to the mechanical and physical properties of toys, being products designed or clearly intended for use in play by children under 14 years of age.
1.2 The requirements of Clause 4 relating to small parts apply to toys intended for children under 36 months of age.
1.3 This standard does not apply to bicycles other than those with a maximum saddle height of 435 mm, to sports equipment, to fireworks, or to scale models for adult collectors.
1.4 This is a demonstration document authored for the SIH26107 prototype and is not an official Indian Standard.

2 References
2.1 DEMO-STD-006 Safety of Toys - Part 2 Migration of Certain Elements.
2.2 DEMO-STD-008 Consumer Products - General Requirements for Marking, Labelling and Instructions.

3 Terminology
3.1 Small part - Any object which fits entirely within the small parts cylinder, being a cylinder of internal diameter 31.7 mm with a base inclined such that the depth is 25.4 mm at the shallowest point and 57.1 mm at the deepest point.
3.2 Age grading - The declared minimum age of the child for whom the toy is intended, as determined by the manufacturer on the basis of play value and the requirements of this standard.
3.3 Reasonably foreseeable abuse - Use of a toy in a manner not intended by the manufacturer but which could be expected of a child of the declared age.

4 Small Parts
4.1 Toys intended for children under 36 months, and removable components of such toys, shall not be small parts as defined in Clause 3.1.
4.2 After the use and abuse tests of Clause 8, a toy intended for children under 36 months shall not liberate any small part.
4.3 Toys intended for children of 36 months and above which contain small parts shall carry the choking hazard warning specified in Clause 9.2.
4.4 Compliance is checked by placing the component, without compressing it, into the small parts cylinder in any orientation.

5 Sharp Edges and Sharp Points
5.1 A toy shall not have accessible sharp edges that present an unreasonable risk of injury during normal use or reasonably foreseeable abuse.
5.2 A toy shall not have accessible sharp points that present an unreasonable risk of injury during normal use or reasonably foreseeable abuse.
5.3 Compliance with Clauses 5.1 and 5.2 is checked before and after the use and abuse tests of Clause 8, using the sharp edge test device and the sharp point test device respectively.
5.4 Functional sharp edges and points necessary for the function of the toy shall be permitted only where the toy is intended for children of 36 months and above and the packaging carries the warning of Clause 9.3.

6 Cords, Straps and Elastics
6.1 Cords and elastics on toys intended for children under 18 months shall not exceed 220 mm in length when measured under a tension of 25 N.
6.2 Toys intended to be strung across a cot or playpen shall carry a warning to remove the toy when the child begins to push up on hands and knees.
6.3 Self retracting cords shall not retract at a rate exceeding the rate declared as safe by the manufacturer and shall not form a loop of perimeter exceeding 380 mm.

7 Enclosures and Entrapment
7.1 Toys which a child can enter and which have a closable opening shall provide a means of opening from the inside using a force not exceeding 45 N.
7.2 Rigid toys with an opening that can admit a child's head shall provide ventilation of not less than the area specified in Table 3 of this standard.

8 Use and Abuse Tests
8.1 Drop test
8.1.1 Toys of mass not exceeding 4.5 kg intended for children under 36 months shall be dropped 10 times from a height of 850 mm onto a steel plate covered with 3 mm thick vinyl composition tile.
8.1.2 After the drop test the toy shall be examined for liberated small parts, for accessible sharp edges and points, and for accessible hazardous filling material.
8.2 Torque test
8.2.1 A torque of 0.34 Nm shall be applied to accessible components over a period of 5 seconds and maintained for 10 seconds, in both directions.
8.3 Tension test
8.3.1 A tensile force of 70 N shall be applied to accessible components over a period of 5 seconds and maintained for 10 seconds.
8.4 Compression test
8.4.1 A compressive force of 110 N shall be applied through a rigid flat disc of diameter 30 mm and maintained for 10 seconds.
8.5 A type test report covering Clauses 8.1 to 8.4, issued by a laboratory competent for these methods, shall be retained by the manufacturer as evidence of conformity.

9 Marking, Labelling and Warnings
9.1 Every toy or its packaging shall carry the manufacturer or importer name and address, the model or batch identification and the declared age grading.
9.2 Toys containing small parts and intended for children of 36 months and above shall carry the warning: Not suitable for children under 36 months. Small parts. Choking hazard. The warning shall be accompanied by an indication of the specific hazard.
9.3 Toys with functional sharp edges or points shall carry a warning identifying the hazard and the minimum age.
9.4 Warnings shall be legible, indelible, and placed on the packaging or on the toy in a position visible to the purchaser at the point of sale.
9.5 Where the toy is covered by a Quality Control Order, conformity marking shall be applied only under a valid licence issued by the certifying authority.

10 Documentation
10.1 The manufacturer shall maintain a technical file containing the design drawings, the bill of materials, the age grading rationale, the test reports of Clause 8 and the chemical migration test reports required by DEMO-STD-006.
